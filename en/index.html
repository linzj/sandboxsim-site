Features

A real macOS, isolated from your Mac, without installing one from scratch.

No download

SandboxSim uses the macOS on this Mac. The first time, it makes a template in about 35 seconds; every new machine starts from it.

Small on disk

System files are shared read-only with your Mac, so a new machine starts at about 0.5 GB and keeps only your changes.

Follows your updates

When your Mac updates macOS, each machine adapts to the new version before it starts, leaving your data alone. You can roll back.

Shared folders

Give a machine folders from your Mac, read-write or read-only. Changes on your Mac show up in the machine at once.

Clipboard and Touch ID

The clipboard syncs both ways. When the machine asks for an administrator, approve with your Mac's Touch ID.

Ready to use

Networking, accelerated graphics, keyboard and mouse, and input methods work out of the box. Set the CPU count, memory and resolution.

What it is for

  • Trying software you do not trust without touching your Mac's files and settings.
  • Testing on a fresh system: installers, first launch, permission prompts.
  • Testing side by side with different accounts and settings.
  • A clean environment for a moment, deleted when you are done.

Requirements and limits

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M-series).
  • macOS 26 or later.
  • The machine runs the same macOS version as your Mac; other versions cannot be installed.
  • No sound and no USB device passthrough yet.
  • Apple's macOS software license agreement allows up to two virtual copies of macOS on a Mac, for software development, testing, and personal non-commercial use. Please use SandboxSim within those terms.

Questions

How do I activate it?

After you pay, the order page and the confirmation email show your licence key. In SandboxSim, choose SandboxSim › 许可证… (Licence), paste the key and click 激活 (Activate). Activation needs the internet once; after that it works offline. A licence activates on 2 Macs of your own; to move it, click 停用这台 Mac (Deactivate this Mac) on the old one.

Does it change my Mac?

No. SandboxSim does not modify the system on your Mac and does not ask for an administrator password. Machines live in ~/Library/Application Support/SandboxSim; deleting a machine deletes its files.

Can a machine see my files?

No, unless you share a folder with it in its settings. A shared folder can be read-only.

How is it different from other virtual machine apps?

A usual macOS virtual machine downloads an installer of more than 10 GB and installs a whole system, tens of GB per machine. SandboxSim reuses your Mac's system, so a machine is ready in seconds and small enough to make and throw away often. The trade-off: it runs only the macOS version your Mac runs.

What happens when I update macOS?

Before a machine first starts on the new version, SandboxSim adapts it, without touching your data inside; you can roll back in its settings. A new major macOS release may need an update of SandboxSim first.

Something went wrong. What now?

Export a diagnostics report from the app and open an issue on GitHub, or write to manjian2006@gmail.com, with the report, your Mac model and macOS version. The report is made only when you export it, and you decide whether to send it. For orders and refunds, write rather than post in a public issue.
