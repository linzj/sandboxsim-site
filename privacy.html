隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

English version below

SandboxSim 不收集你的个人信息。App 里没有广告、统计或跟踪代码，也不需要注册账户。

App 什么时候联网

  • 获取启动文件：如果这台 Mac 上缺少当前 macOS 版本的虚拟机启动文件，App 会查询苹果的服务器（mesu.apple.com）和 ipsw.me，找到包含该版本的安装包，然后只从苹果的服务器下载需要的那一部分。这些请求带有 Mac 的型号标识（例如 Mac16,1）和 macOS 版本号，不包含你的个人信息。
  • 试用和许可证：开始试用时以及试用期间每天一次，App 会把一个设备标识发送给我们的许可证服务器（运行在 Cloudflare 上），由它记下这台 Mac 开始试用的日期。激活、每天核验和停用许可证时，App 会把许可证密钥、Mac 的型号标识和设备标识发送给许可证服务器，由它向经销商 Creem 核验。设备标识是由这台 Mac 的硬件 UUID 计算出的哈希值，不能反推出硬件 UUID，只用来区分你的不同 Mac。
  • 检查更新：App 每天从本网站读取一次新版本的信息（Sparkle），不发送任何个人信息。
  • 虚拟机的网络：虚拟机里的程序和主机上的其他程序一样，经由你的 Mac 上网。这些流量不经过我们。

诊断报告

只有你在 App 里选择导出时才会生成，保存在你选择的位置。报告包含 App 版本、Mac 型号、CPU 核数和内存、macOS 版本、兼容性检查结果、模板和虚拟机的设置（共享文件夹只记个数），以及引擎日志的末尾部分；主文件夹的路径以 ~ 代替。是否把它发给我们，由你决定。

购买

付款由我们的经销商处理，我们接触不到你的银行卡信息。经销商会向我们提供完成订单所需的信息，例如姓名、邮箱、国家或地区和订单详情。我们只用这些信息交付许可证、提供售后和处理退款，不会出售或用于广告。

本网站

本网站托管在 GitHub Pages，不使用 Cookie 或统计脚本。GitHub 可能记录访问者的 IP 地址等技术信息，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

SandboxSim does not collect personal information. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and needs no account.

When the app goes online

  • Boot files: if this Mac lacks the virtual machine boot files for its macOS version, the app asks Apple's servers (mesu.apple.com) and ipsw.me which installer holds that version, then downloads only the part it needs from Apple's servers. These requests carry the Mac's model identifier (such as Mac16,1) and macOS version, and no personal information.
  • Trial and licences: when the trial begins, and once a day during it, the app sends a device identifier to our licence server (on Cloudflare), which records when this Mac's trial began. When you activate, deactivate, and once a day, the app sends the licence key, the Mac's model identifier and the device identifier to the licence server, which checks them with our reseller, Creem. The device identifier is a hash of the Mac's hardware UUID; the UUID cannot be recovered from it, and it serves only to tell your Macs apart.
  • Update checks: once a day the app reads information about new versions from this website (Sparkle), sending no personal information.
  • The machines' network: programs inside a machine go online through your Mac like any other app. That traffic does not pass through us.

Diagnostics reports

A report is made only when you export one from the app, and is saved where you choose. It holds the app version, Mac model, CPU count and memory, macOS version, compatibility check results, template and machine settings (shared folders only as a count), and the end of the engine's log, with your home folder's path written as ~. You decide whether to send it to us.

Purchases

Payments are handled by our reseller; we never see your card details. The reseller gives us what an order needs, such as your name, email, country or region and order details. We use it only to deliver your licence, provide support and process refunds, and never sell it or use it for advertising.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub may log technical information such as visitors' IP addresses; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
