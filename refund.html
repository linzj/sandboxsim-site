English version below

14 天无理由退款

购买后 14 天内，如果你对 SandboxSim 不满意，可以申请全额退款，不需要说明理由。

怎么申请

通过购买确认邮件中经销商提供的订单链接或客服渠道申请，并注明订单号。请不要在 GitHub 的公开 issue 里贴订单信息。

退款之后

退款完成后，对应的许可证失效，App 回到未激活状态；你的虚拟机和其中的数据不受影响。

超过 14 天

超过 14 天的订单原则上不予退款。如果 SandboxSim 因自身缺陷无法在符合系统要求的 Mac 上使用，而我们又无法修复，请联系我们个案处理。

到账时间

退款由经销商原路退回，到账时间取决于经销商和你的付款方式。

Refund Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

14 days, no questions asked

If you are not happy with SandboxSim, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, without giving a reason.

How to ask

Use the order link or support contact the reseller gives in your order confirmation email, with your order number. Please do not post order details in a public GitHub issue.

After a refund

The licence is revoked and the app returns to its unactivated state. Your machines and their data are not affected.

After 14 days

Orders older than 14 days are generally not refunded. If a defect in SandboxSim stops it from working on a Mac that meets the requirements and we cannot fix it, contact us and we will look at your case.

Timing

Refunds go back to your original payment method through the reseller; how long it takes depends on the reseller and the payment method.
